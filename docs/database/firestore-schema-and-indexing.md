### `invoices` & `financial_journals`
IRC PNG GST compliant tax invoices with balanced double-entry ledger.
- Debit: `CASH_BANK` or `ACCOUNTS_RECEIVABLE`
- Credit: `TRANSPORT_REVENUE`
- Credit: `GST_PAYABLE` (10% to Internal Revenue Commission)
